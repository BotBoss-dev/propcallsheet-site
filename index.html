What you get

Everything on one chart.

Calls

BUY and SELL labels

BUY prints under the candle, SELL above it. Each label has its own strength line, like Q 7/10 · 9/17 agree: the call's quality score and how many of the 17 built-in setups agree. Calls are decided on bar close.

Trade box

Entry, stop, TP1 / TP2 / TP3

Every call draws a box with entry, stop and three target tags. A dashed target path shows where the targets sit. It is an illustration, not a forecast.

Structure

FVG, BOS / ChoCH, supply and demand

Fair value gaps, breaks of structure, changes of character and supply/demand zones are marked. Hover any tag for a plain explanation of what it means.

Dashboard

Top-right summary

Bias on 1H, 4H and Daily, the current session, the next move for an open call, today's calls and paper-tested stats, labelled "Paper-tested: hypothetical results".

Daily layer

RSI(2) and IBS paper calls

Separate daily calls from two classic rules, tagged PAPER on the chart. They are shown for study and stay marked as paper calls.

News

Skips Fed (FOMC) days

No new calls from 24 hours before to 2 hours after a scheduled Fed (FOMC) decision. You can switch it off in settings.

Alerts

TradingView alerts

Set alerts for new calls, target and stop events, so you hear about a call without watching the chart.

Telegram

Private signals channel

Calls also post to a private members-only Telegram channel, so they reach your phone as a normal notification.

How to install on TradingView

  1. Open the indicator file the bot sent and copy all of it.
  2. Go to tradingview.com on a computer or phone browser (the Pine Editor is not in the TradingView phone app) and open a chart.
  3. Open the Pine Editor at the bottom of the chart.
  4. Select everything in the editor (Ctrl+A, or Cmd+A on Mac) and paste (Ctrl+V).
  5. Click Save, then Add to chart.
  6. Pick MNQ, MES or MBT. Calls are tuned for the 1H chart, which matches the Telegram channel.
  7. For alerts: Create alert, choose PCS Ω as the condition, pick the call or event you want.

Works on any TradingView account, free or paid. New version? Send /indicator to the bot and repeat steps 1 to 5.

Can I use it on my phone?

Installing needs the Pine Editor on tradingview.com (a computer, or a phone browser in desktop mode). Telegram calls work on any phone.

What does "Q 7/10 · 9/17 agree" mean?

Q is the call's quality score out of 10 (it blends how many setups agree, higher-timeframe trend, market type, session and stretch). 9/17 is how many of the 17 built-in setups vote with the call. Neither is a probability or a promise about the trade.

Does it repaint?

Calls are decided when the bar closes, so a printed call does not move or disappear afterwards.
