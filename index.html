FAQ

Questions

What do I get when I pay?

Access to the private PropCallSheet Telegram channel, where signal calls for MNQ, MES and MBT are posted with entry, stop loss and take profit levels, plus the PropCallSheet TradingView indicator free.

How do I get the indicator?

It comes free with your purchase. After you send /claim, @PropCallSheetSignals_bot sends you the indicator file with simple steps to add it to TradingView. It works on any TradingView account, including free ones. Send /indicator to the bot any time for the latest version.

What is the Founding 50?

Our launch offer. The first 50 buyers pay $50 once for access to the private channel for as long as PropCallSheet operates it, on the same terms as the $499 lifetime plan. Checkout stops after the 50th sale and the offer does not come back.

How do I get into the channel after paying?

Open @PropCallSheetSignals_bot on Telegram and send /claim followed by the email you used at checkout. The bot checks your payment and replies with a one-time invite link. The link works once, so don't share it.

Do I need Telegram or a paid TradingView plan?

You need the free Telegram app to join the channel. The indicator works on any TradingView account, free or paid; paid TradingView plans only add more indicators per chart and more alerts.

Which markets are covered?

Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ), Micro E-mini S&P 500 (MES) and CME Micro Bitcoin (MBT). The indicator also plots on the full-size contracts, since price action is the same.

Does it place trades for me?

No. The channel and the indicator are analysis tools. You decide whether to take a trade, and you place and manage orders with your own broker.

Can I use it for prop firm evaluations?

Many prop firms allow third-party signals and indicators, but rules differ. Check your firm's terms on signal services and copy trading before using it on a funded or evaluation account. We don't sell, fund or evaluate prop firm accounts.

How do I cancel?

Founding 50 and Lifetime are single payments, so there is nothing to cancel. Weekly and monthly plans are coming soon; once they open, you can cancel from the manage subscription page by signing in with your checkout email.

I paid but have no access

Make sure you sent /claim with the exact email you used at checkout. If the bot still can't find your payment, or your invite link stopped working, email swainwebstudio@gmail.com from your checkout email.
