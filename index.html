FAQ

Questions

Do I need a paid TradingView plan?

No. The indicator is invite-only and works on any TradingView account. Paid TradingView plans add more simultaneous alerts and indicators per chart, which some traders prefer.

Which markets are covered?

Micro E-mini Nasdaq-100 (MNQ), Micro E-mini S&P 500 (MES) and CME Micro Bitcoin (MBT). The signals also plot on the full-size contracts, since price action is the same.

Does it place trades for me?

No. It is an analysis and alert tool. You decide whether to take a trade, and you place and manage orders with your own broker.

Can I use it for prop firm evaluations?

Many prop firms allow third-party indicators and alerts, but rules differ. Check your firm's terms on signal services and copy trading before using it on a funded or evaluation account.

How do I cancel?

Send /billing to the bot for your Stripe link to cancel, change card or see invoices. Access stays active until the end of the period you paid for.

I paid on Stripe but have no access

Send /claim and the email you used at checkout to the bot, for example /claim you@example.com.

What is your win rate?

We do not publish performance figures. Past results, real or simulated, do not predict future results, and any number we quoted could mislead you. Judge the signals on your own chart before risking money.
